Anonymous Incident Registration

Your safety and privacy are our highest priority. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, or any personal identity details. Your complaint is forwarded directly to the police station of the respective district, and you will receive a Unique Case Code to follow up privately.

Location of Occurrence

Where did the incident happen? This determines which district police station receives your case.

e.g. Near Bus Terminal 4, Main Market road opposite City Mall, inside parking lot, etc.
The case will be directly assigned to this district's police department.
2

Incident Timing

Specify the date and approximate time when the event took place.

3

Register the Complaint

Type and record the complaint statement in your own words.

This statement will be officially submitted as your anonymous incident report.
4

Case Investigation Details

The following key question helps the investigating officers take effective action.

Helpful details you may include if known: Perpetrator's physical appearance (height, clothing, age, marks/tattoos), vehicle type/color/number fragment, direction they escaped, physical evidence left behind, presence of CCTV cameras, or witnesses nearby.
Share only what you feel comfortable sharing. Every detail helps law enforcement reconstruct the incident.
Strict No-PII Policy: No name, email, phone number, or IP address is tracked or saved with this report.
